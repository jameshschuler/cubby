Privacy policy

Your financial plans are private.

Effective November 6, 2026

What Cubby stores

Cubby stores the savings goals, progress entries, optional notes, income settings, and savings targets that you enter. This information is stored locally on your device and is included in your own device or iCloud backups.

What Cubby collects

Cubby does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information. Cubby does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, user accounts, or cloud synchronization.

Exporting data

When you select Export Data, Cubby creates a JSON file containing your goals, progress events, and settings. The file is shared only through the iOS share sheet, only to a destination you choose, and is removed from Cubby once sharing finishes.

Deleting data

You can delete individual goals from within Cubby, or remove all of your Cubby data with Erase All Data in Settings. Deleting the app also removes the data that Cubby stores on your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Cubby's data practices change. The effective date above indicates when it was last revised.
