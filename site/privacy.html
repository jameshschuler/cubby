Privacy policy

Your financial plans are private.

Effective August 26, 2026

What Cubby stores

Cubby stores the savings goals, progress entries, optional notes, income settings, and savings targets that you enter. This information is stored locally on your device.

What Cubby collects

Cubby does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information. Cubby does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, user accounts, or cloud synchronization.

Exporting data

When you select Export Data, Cubby creates a JSON file containing your goals, progress events, and settings on your device. The file is shared only through the iOS share sheet and only to a destination you choose.

Deleting data

You can delete individual goals from within Cubby. Deleting the app from your device removes the data that Cubby stores locally.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Cubby's data practices change. The effective date above indicates when it was last revised.
