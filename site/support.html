Support

Here when you need a hand.

For help with Cubby, email hello@getcubby.xyz. Include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

Where is my data stored?

Goals, progress, and settings are stored locally on your device. Cubby does not require an account.

How do I export my data?

Open Settings in Cubby and select Export Data. The app creates a JSON file and opens the standard iOS share sheet.

How do I remove my data?

Delete individual goals in the Goals tab. To remove all Cubby data from a device, delete the app from iOS.
